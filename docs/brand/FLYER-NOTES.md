# Brand notes from the Char'd flyer (received 2026-09-25)

Source: "Monday Pizza" flyer shared in chat. Drop the original image in this folder as `flyer-monday-pizza.jpg` when convenient.

## Look
- Background: near-black charcoal with a scratched/grunge texture, wood-fired oven glow behind the food.
- Accent: golden amber (approx #F2A33A), used for headline words, brush-stroke highlight blocks, the ORDER NOW button, and torn-paper edges.
- Text on dark: warm cream (approx #F3EBDD) for the big word PIZZA and body copy.
- Secondary accent: burnt orange / brick red line divider (approx #C8551E).
- Typography: heavy condensed all-caps display type with a distressed/stamped texture for headlines (MONDAY), clean geometric sans (Poppins-like) for body and labels, wide letter-spacing on small caps labels ("$25 PER PIE  16 IN").
- Shapes: torn paper edges between sections, rough brush-stroke highlight boxes behind text.
- Logo: brick arch with a flame inside, wordmark CHAR'D in distressed cream caps beneath it.

## Facts on the flyer
- Classic Char'd Pizza: crispy thin crust with a touch of char, fresh tomato sauce, melted mozzarella, oven-crisped basil brushed in garlic avocado oil.
- Pizza Bianca: mozzarella, creamy ricotta, basil, shaved parmesan, drizzle of garlic oil.
- $25 per pie, 16 inch.
- The flyer has a hashgacha line. Aaron said 2026-09-25: no hashgacha anymore, do NOT show it on the site.

## Apply
- Public site: dark theme, amber CTA, cream headline type, grunge texture, torn edges.
- Admin: keep light and legible; borrow the amber as the accent color and the charcoal for the brand mark only.
